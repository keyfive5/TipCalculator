# Tipwise Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Tipwise is a tip calculator. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Tipwise does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal data.

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- No network requests are made by the app.

## What stays on your phone

Tipwise remembers your preferences (tip percentage, number of people, currency, tax setting,
appearance and haptics) in the app's local storage on your device so they are there next time.
This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Sharing

If you tap **Share**, Tipwise hands a plain-text summary of the bill to the iOS share sheet.
Where it goes from there is up to you; Tipwise does not see or store it.

## Children

Tipwise is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
